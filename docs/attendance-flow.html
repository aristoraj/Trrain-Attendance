Step 1
🔐 Login Detection
The system automatically reads who is logged into Zoho Creator. No separate login is needed for the attendance tool.
Step 2
✅ Access Check
Only trainers with the Face Recognition feature enabled can use the tool. Unauthorized users see a clear message to contact admin.
Step 3
👁️ Blink Test
The student is asked to blink naturally. This confirms a real person is in front of the camera — not a photograph being held up.
Step 4
🤖 Anti-Spoof Check
An AI model checks that the captured image is a live face — blocking attempts to fool the system with printed photos or phone screens.
Step 5
🧠 Face Matching
The captured face is compared against all student photos stored in Zoho Creator. Matching takes less than 1 second.
Step 6
🎉 Attendance Marked
Once a student is recognised, their attendance is confirmed instantly on screen and quietly saved to Zoho Creator in the background — no waiting.
Duplicate Guard
💛 Already Marked
If the same student is scanned again on the same day, the system shows a friendly message and does not create a duplicate record.
Step 7
🔄 Next Student
The camera resets automatically after each successful scan. The trainer does not need to press any button — just wave the next student forward.
